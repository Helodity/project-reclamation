INTRO

It was known by many names. The Savior of Life. The Golden Core. The Devil’s Lure. PNF-404.
Now, it only goes by one: Wasteland.

Eager to exploit the planet’s wealth, travelers arrived by the thousands. Shipments back left even faster. Within months, planet-wide infrastructure had been funded and began construction.

Eventually, the scavengers supply ran dry. Without their source of income, they turned towards the rest of the planet. The flora was stripped from the dirt. The creatures who survived the initial construction were slaughtered and sent back for profit. Everything must go, and just as fast as the travelers arrived, they left, leaving a husk of what was.
